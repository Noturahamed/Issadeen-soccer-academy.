Our Story

Building the Future of Football

Founded in 2025, ISA Football Academy was born out of a profound passion for the beautiful game and a deep commitment to youth development. We started with just a handful of kids and a dream, and today we're one of the premier football academies in the region.

Our philosophy is simple: we don't just create great football players; we help shape exceptional individuals. Discipline, teamwork, respect, and resilience are at the core of everything we do on and off the pitch.
